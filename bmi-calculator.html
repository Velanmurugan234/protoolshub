📐 BMI Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²)

📊 BMI Categories (WHO)

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0 and above

⚠️ BMI Limitations

BMI does not distinguish between muscle and fat. Athletes may have high BMI but low body fat. Always consult a healthcare provider.
